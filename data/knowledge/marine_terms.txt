Marine Terms and Definitions

SST (Sea Surface Temperature):
SST stands for Sea Surface Temperature. It refers to the water temperature near the ocean's surface. Changes in SST reveal thermal fronts, upwelling of nutrients, and ocean currents where fish commonly feed.

PFZ (Potential Fishing Zone):
PFZ stands for Potential Fishing Zone. It indicates ocean sectors where favorable temperature, chlorophyll levels, and currents make fish schools more likely to congregate.

IMBL (International Maritime Boundary Line):
IMBL stands for International Maritime Boundary Line. It marks the legal international maritime border dividing the territorial waters and exclusive economic zones of neighboring nations (such as India and Sri Lanka). Fishermen must strictly avoid crossing the IMBL without official clearance.

EEZ (Exclusive Economic Zone):
EEZ stands for Exclusive Economic Zone. Under international maritime law, an EEZ extends up to 200 nautical miles from a coastal nation's baseline, giving that nation sovereign rights over marine resources and fishing.

Chlorophyll-a:
Chlorophyll-a is a natural green pigment found in microscopic marine plants called phytoplankton. Higher chlorophyll concentration indicates rich biological productivity and abundant plankton, attracting baitfish and commercial fish like sardines, mackerel, and tuna.

Wave Height and Significant Wave Height:
Wave height is the vertical distance between the wave crest (highest point) and trough (lowest point). Significant Wave Height is the average height of the highest one-third of waves, representing what a boat will experience at sea.

Wind Speed and Beaufort Scale:
Wind speed measures the velocity of surface air movement, usually reported in knots or kilometers per hour. Strong sustained winds over 25 knots generate rough seas and steep chop dangerous for small craft.

Swell Waves vs Wind Waves:
Swell waves are long, rolling waves generated by distant ocean storms that can travel across open ocean for thousands of miles. Wind waves are local choppy waves created by immediate local winds.
