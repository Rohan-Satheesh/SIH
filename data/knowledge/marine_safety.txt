Marine Safety and Seamanship Guidelines

Pre-Departure Safety Checklist:
Before leaving port or venturing into the open sea, fishermen must:
1. Verify official weather forecasts, wind speeds, wave heights, and storm/cyclone warnings from IMD and INCOIS.
2. Ensure mandatory life jackets and life buoys for all crew members on board.
3. Check communication equipment (VHF marine radio, AIS transponder, satellite distress beacons/DAT, mobile phones with waterproof pouches).
4. Verify navigation lights, GPS compass, emergency torches, and signaling flares.
5. Carry sufficient emergency fresh water, rations, first-aid kit, and spare engine fuel/filters.

Severe Weather and Cyclone Protocols:
Small craft and mechanized fishing boats should never venture to sea during:
- Gale or strong wind warnings exceeding 40-50 km/h (22-27 knots).
- High wave or rough sea alerts with waves exceeding 2.5 to 3.0 meters.
- Squall, depression, or cyclone warnings.
If weather suddenly deteriorates while fishing at sea, immediately secure gear, don life jackets, inform the harbor, and navigate toward the nearest designated safe harbor or shelter.

International Boundary Awareness:
Keep an active GPS watch when operating near coastal border areas. Maintain safe buffer distances from the International Maritime Boundary Line (IMBL) to prevent accidental border transgressions.
