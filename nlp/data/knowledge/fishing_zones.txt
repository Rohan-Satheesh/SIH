Potential Fishing Zones

Potential Fishing Zones, or PFZs, are areas where fish
may be more abundant.

PFZ information can be estimated using ocean conditions
such as sea surface temperature, chlorophyll concentration,
ocean color, and ocean fronts.

PFZ information is a recommendation and should not be
treated as a guarantee of fish availability.

Fishermen should combine PFZ information with local knowledge,
weather conditions, boat capacity, and safety warnings.
