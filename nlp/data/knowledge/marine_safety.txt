Marine Safety

Before going to sea, fishermen should check weather forecasts,
wind speed, wave height, rainfall, and cyclone warnings.

Small boats should avoid going to sea during severe weather,
high waves, strong winds, or cyclone warnings.

Fishermen should carry life jackets, communication devices,
drinking water, first-aid supplies, and emergency equipment.

If weather conditions suddenly worsen, return to shore
or move to the nearest safe harbor.
